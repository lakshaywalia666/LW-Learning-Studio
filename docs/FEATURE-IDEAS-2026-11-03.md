# LW Learning Studio — feature idea catalogue

3 November 2026. Proposal backlog for the current 1.1.0 app, based on the local implementation and feature documentation. These ideas have not been implemented by this catalogue. Existing features include single-task Study, course authoring/import/export, exercises and reviews, notebook pages/tags/nesting, PDF annotations, voice recordings, five worlds, profiles, design presets, personal reminders, backups and optional encrypted snapshot sync.

Keep the ordinary Study screen to one task, its explanation and Done. Additional tools should be optional, in their relevant tools or settings. Personal reminders and habits must not write Study completions, minutes or forest progress. Fresh installations should remain empty. A larger APK is acceptable; smoothness, data integrity and understandable flows take priority.

Starred ideas are strong candidates for the next releases, subject to effort and device checks. Account services, collaboration, a hosted course library and hosted sync require explicit infrastructure planning. AI should be optional, show its data-use/cost model, preserve source content, and let people review generated drafts. On-device AI availability depends on hardware and platform support.

## Home and quick capture

1. ★ **Capture inbox:** Collect unsorted ideas and organize them later.
2. ★ **Quick Add anywhere:** Open one sheet to create a note, reminder or study item.
3. **Home favourites:** Pin chosen notes, courses and reminders to Home.
4. **Rearrange Home:** Choose the order and visibility of Home cards.
5. **Choose the opening screen:** Start on Home, Study, Reminders or Notes.
6. **Morning briefing:** See a short overview of appointments, reminders and planned study.
7. **Evening wrap-up:** Review the day and choose what to carry forward.
8. **Recently opened:** Jump back to recently visited content across the app.
9. **Saved searches:** Keep useful filters such as unfinished work tagged Personal.
10. ★ **Search personal reminders:** Include reminder titles, notes and completed items in global search.

## Reminders

11. ★ **Type dates naturally:** Enter 'Call Alex tomorrow at 6 pm' and preview the date.
12. ★ **Reminder snooze:** Delay a personal alert by minutes, hours or a chosen date.
13. ★ **Advanced repeat rules:** Repeat every N days, on weekdays or on the last Friday.
14. **Repeat end conditions:** Stop after a date or a chosen number of occurrences.
15. **Repeat after completion:** Schedule the next occurrence relative to when Done was actually tapped.
16. **Separate deadline and alert:** Distinguish when something is due from when to notify.
17. **Multiple alerts:** Choose an early warning and another alert near the due time.
18. **Reminder organization:** Add optional lists, colour labels and priorities.
19. **Reminder attachments:** Attach a picture, document or useful link.
20. **Bulk reminder actions:** Move dates, change lists or archive selected reminders together.
