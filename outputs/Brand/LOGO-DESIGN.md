# LW logo — version 1

Created 3 November 2026 with the built-in image_gen tool. This is a new design asset; existing launcher icons and APKs have not been replaced.

Asset: `lw-logo-v1.png`, square PNG with a full-bleed blue background.

The pearl-white folded LW monogram reflects the app's glass interface. Broad strokes and a blue background preserve contrast at small sizes. The square background permits platform-specific icon masking.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand.

Asset type: final square app-icon logo master for LW Learning Studio, a personal learning, notes and reminders app with an elegant liquid-glass interface.

Primary request: design one exceptionally polished, distinctive LW monogram logo. The capital letters L and W must be clearly recognizable, custom drawn as one cohesive symbol using broad, softly folded ribbon strokes. The subtle fold can suggest the pages of an open book, but keep the LW lettering the dominant reading. Quiet, precise, premium, friendly and confident.

Composition: one centered large mark occupying roughly 62 percent of a square canvas, generous equal padding, straight-on orthographic view. Full-bleed rich cobalt / royal blue background, subtly luminous around the center. Background fills the entire square to the edges, without an outer frame or a pre-rounded app tile. The symbol is luminous pearl-white glass with restrained ice-blue edge refraction, substantial opaque core and controlled soft depth so it remains very legible when reduced to a small phone icon. Crisp silhouette, balanced optical spacing, beautifully resolved ribbon joins. Gentle soft directional light, refined rather than flashy.

Text (verbatim): "LW" as the monogram only. No other text.

Constraints: create the actual standalone logo asset, not a presentation, not a phone mockup, not a logo sheet, not a screenshot. One square image, high resolution, clean background. No stock education emblem, no graduation cap, no leaf, no extra book pictogram, no bells, no checkmark, no star or sparkle decoration, no watermark, no tiny linework, no metallic chrome, no rainbow sheen. Preserve strong recognition of L and W.

## App icon variants — 3 November 2026

The original opaque glass logo is included in the web app as `icons/lw-logo.png` for full-colour install/touch artwork. `icons/lw-app.svg` and the Android `lw_mark.xml` / `lw_monochrome.xml` are vector adaptations for clean small-size launch artwork and adaptive masking. Android 13 and later receive a monochrome layer. The original generation prompt and built-in generation provenance are above; native/vector variants are code assets. Transparent extraction experiments were excluded from the app.
