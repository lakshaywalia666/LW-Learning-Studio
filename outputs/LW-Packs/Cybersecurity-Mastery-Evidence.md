# Expert-track evidence milestones

Prepared 6 November 2026. The intended outcome is advanced independent practice and research in chosen specialties, supported by broad system knowledge. Progress counters record your own work; they do not certify competence.

## After sections 01–18

Explain systems, networking, identity and risk; administer disposable hosts and defend a scoped assessment method without relying on memorized commands.

## After sections 19–50

Build and assess a small system, verify fixes, produce useful telemetry, investigate a synthetic incident and demonstrate recovery. Preserve limitations and failed hypotheses.

## After sections 51–82

Choose at least two specialties for independent depth. Reproduce published research, diagnose unfamiliar failures and verify root-cause improvements on exact platform versions.

## After sections 83–97

Design a falsifiable specialist study, evaluate realistic counterexamples, build or improve a research tool and obtain informed independent review.

## After sections 98–100

Defend a blind case, a multi-domain integration project and a meaningful original contribution or rigorous replication before qualified reviewers. Revise the work in response to criticism.

## Portfolio requirements

For each major claim preserve the question, prior work, exact versions, authorized scope, threat assumptions, environment, repeatable method, expected and actual observations, counterexamples, limitations, fix or improvement and independent feedback. Keep secrets and private information out of shared artifacts.

A substantial research result can be a defensible new vulnerability finding, a meaningful tool or method, an important independently replicated result or a rigorous negative result. Novelty must be compared with existing work. An original result is not required in every specialty; expert depth is built and demonstrated in selected fields.

## Honest completion test

Can you handle a new scoped case without a supplied answer, distinguish observation from inference, validate a correction, defend your assumptions and revise a conclusion under criticism? If not, revisit the relevant work. Peer review and real operational feedback are essential for consequential claims. There is no permanent end of cybersecurity knowledge; maintain sources and repeat unfamiliar work.
